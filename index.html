Ohajiki Counter

Multi-Class Manual Cell & Particle Counter for Android

Android App

A streamlined, high-precision tally counter optimized for histological analysis, immunohistochemistry (IHC) evaluations (e.g., Ki-67 labeling index, ER/PgR, p53), and general laboratory cell counting directly from microscopic images or live fields.

#
# #
  • Multi-channel differential counting with custom label configurations.
  • Automatic labeling index (%) calculation for fast pathological estimation.
  • Clean, distraction-free UI designed for speed and microscopic workflows.
  • No telemetry, no tracking, and fully operable offline.
